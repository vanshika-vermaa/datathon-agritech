DATA NOTES & METHODOLOGY

  • Crop names standardized across English, Hindi and Punjabi variants (e.g. गेहूं / Gehun → Wheat; Sarso → Mustard; Ganne → Sugarcane; Narma → Cotton; Makki → Maize; चावल / Chawal → Rice).
  • Arrival quantities converted to quintals from a mix of Kilograms and Metric Tonnes (1 Tonne = 10 Qtl, 1 Qtl = 100 Kg).
  • Transport distances converted from miles to kilometers where the source unit was miles; transit hours had already been corrected for negative/impossible values in the source file.
  • Weather sensor rainfall is normalized to an average per active sensor per district-day, since raw totals reflect sums across a varying number of sensors (23–40 per day).
  • Arrival-based metrics are scoped to Jan – Aug 2026, the window with recorded arrival activity; price, MSP and weather records extend into December 2026.
  • All records are synthetic data generated for the TransOrg AgentIQ Datathon and do not reflect real mandi activity.
